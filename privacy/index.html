Shuffle Duel — Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 21, 2026

Shuffle Duel ("the app") is published by BEBE BIO Inc. This policy explains what happens to information when you play.

Information we collect

None. The app has no sign-in, no analytics, no advertising SDK and no crash-reporting service. We do not collect, store or sell personal data, and the app does not track you across apps or websites.

Information stored on your device

Your progress (level, rank, coins, unlocked items, statistics), your player name and your sound and vibration settings are saved locally on your device so the game can continue where you left off. This data never leaves your device and is deleted when you delete the app.

Purchases

The optional VIP subscription and Remove Ads are sold through Apple's App Store. Apple processes the payment; we do not receive your payment details. The app checks your purchases with Apple's StoreKit to unlock what you bought. Manage or cancel subscriptions in your Apple Account settings. Apple's own privacy policy applies to App Store purchases.

Breaks between games

Breaks and the banner show promotions for features of Shuffle Duel itself. They are built into the app and do not contact any advertising network.

Children

The app is suitable for all ages and does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.

How do I throw?

Swipe up on the table. A longer swipe slides the puck farther. Stop it in the 1, 2 or 3 zone to score; pucks that fall off the table score 0.

I bought VIP or Remove Ads on another device

Open Settings in the game and tap Restore Purchases, signed in with the Apple Account you bought with.

How do I cancel VIP?

Open the Settings app › your name › Subscriptions › Shuffle Duel VIP › Cancel Subscription, or tap Manage Subscription in the game's Settings while VIP is active. Cancel at least 24 hours before the renewal date to avoid the next charge.

My progress is gone

Progress is stored on the device only. Deleting the app deletes it; purchases can be restored.
